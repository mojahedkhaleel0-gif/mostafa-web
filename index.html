NBR accountant

Professional accounting and bookkeeping for small businesses — invoices, payments, reports, reminders, and optional cloud backup.

Developer: Renad Software

About the app

NBR accountant is an accounting application designed to help you manage financial records efficiently and securely. The app supports daily workflows such as creating invoices, tracking payments, generating account statements, and reviewing reports.

Key features

Invoices & returns
  • Create customer and supplier invoices
  • Credit notes / returns with optional linkage to original invoices
  • Tax/VAT calculations (when enabled in settings)
Payments & balances
  • Record payments and allocate receipts
  • Handle overpayments as customer/supplier credit (advance)
  • Track outstanding balances
Reminders & notifications
  • Due invoice reminders
  • Payment reminders and smart notifications (optional)
Scan & capture (optional)
  • Barcode scanning and OCR to help with data entry
  • Uses the device camera only when you choose to scan
Backup & restore (optional)
  • Google Drive AppData backup/restore (opt-in)
  • Requires Google Sign-In only if you enable cloud backup
Data control
  • Export and delete data from within the app
  • Local-first storage by default
Note: Some features (such as cloud backup, OCR, barcode scanning, and reminders) are optional and can be enabled or disabled in the app settings.
