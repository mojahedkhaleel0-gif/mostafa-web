Privacy Policy

App: NBR accountant — Developer: Renad Software
Support: renadsoftware@gmail.com
Last updated: 2026-05-14

This Privacy Policy explains how Renad Software (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use NBR accountant (the “App”).

The App is designed to be local-first: most accounting data you enter (such as invoices, payments, and records) is stored on your device. Some features are optional and may involve network services (for example, Google Drive backup/restore).

1) Information we handle

A. Information you enter (business/accounting data)

The App may process and store locally on your device:

  • Invoices, credit notes/returns, items, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes
  • Payments, receipts/advances, allocations, write-offs
  • Customers/suppliers (names, contact details if you choose to enter them)
  • Company profile data you configure (company name, logo, tax number, address)
  • Reports and statements generated inside the App

B. Google account information (only if you use Google Sign-In)

If you choose to enable cloud backup/restore, the App may use Google Sign-In and access limited Google account information such as:

  • Email address
  • Google account identifier

The App requests access for Google Drive AppData storage to back up and restore your app data (when enabled).

C. Camera input (only when you choose to scan)

The App may use your device camera for optional scanning features such as OCR (text recognition) and barcode scanning. Camera data is used to help you capture information and speed up data entry.

D. Notifications

The App may send notifications for reminders (e.g., due invoices, payment reminders) if you enable notifications and grant the relevant permission on your device.

2) How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide core accounting functions (invoices, payments, statements, reports)
  • Calculate totals, taxes, and balances based on your entries and settings
  • Provide reminders and notifications (optional)
  • Back up and restore app data to Google Drive AppData (optional, user-controlled)
  • Improve app stability (e.g., to diagnose errors if you report issues to support)

3) Data storage and where your data lives

  • Local storage: By default, your accounting data is stored on your device.
  • Cloud backup (optional): If you enable Google Drive backup, the App may upload backup data to your Google Drive AppData area. This area is intended for app-specific backups.

4) Sharing of data

We do not sell your personal data. The App may interact with third-party services only to provide specific features you enable:

  • Google services (Google Sign-In and Google Drive AppData for backup/restore, if enabled)
  • Google Play services used by on-device ML features (for example, model delivery for scanning, depending on device configuration)

These services process data under their own policies. You can review Google’s privacy information here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5) Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Camera: used only for scanning features when you choose to scan.
  • Notifications: used to send reminders when enabled.
  • Internet / Network state: used for Google Sign-In, backup/restore, and related online features.

You can deny permissions; some features may not work without them, but core local features may still be available.

6) Data retention

  • On-device: Data remains on your device until you delete it from within the App or uninstall the App.
  • Google Drive backups (if enabled): Backups remain in your Google Drive AppData until you delete them via the App’s backup settings or your Google account tools (as applicable).

7) Data deletion and export

The App includes options to export and delete your data from within the application settings. If you need assistance, contact support: renadsoftware@gmail.com

8) Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data stored and processed by the App. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure. We recommend using device security features (screen lock, biometrics) and keeping your OS updated.

9) Children’s privacy

NBR accountant is intended for business/accounting use and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the “Last updated” date when changes are made.
